1. Executive Project Summary & Core Mission

The Urban Intelligence & Transit ADAS Platform transforms existing city transit bus fleets into continuous, mobile urban scanners. Operating via non-intrusive dashboard cameras and synchronized GPS receivers, the onboard edge computing box autonomously detects, classifies, and geo-tags eight distinct urban hazards—including road potholes, stagnant waterlogging pools, traffic bottlenecks, school zone pedestrian safety violations, rash driving speed offenders (ANPR), illegal garbage heaps, and traffic signal blackouts.

By replacing dedicated, multi-crore specialized survey vans with ordinary city buses running their daily scheduled routes, the system achieves over 90% daily road coverage at a fraction of traditional infrastructure costs, converting municipal maintenance from a reactive citizen-complaint model into an automated, proactive civic intelligence grid.

2. End-to-End System Workflow (How It Works Step-by-Step)

The system operates in a seamless, automated 5-stage processing pipeline:

Step 1: Automated In-Transit Data Ingestion & GPS Telemetry Sync

As the bus starts its route, the onboard edge box automatically triggers on ignition. A forward-facing HD camera records the road corridor while an active GPS receiver logs latitude, longitude, transit speed, and heading vector at 1-second intervals. Corridors are automatically geo-fenced (e.g., Gorakhpur Smart Corridor, Bengaluru Transit, Delhi Arterial).

Step 2: Multi-Sensor Edge AI Inference & Hazard Localization

Video frames are passed to the master_pipeline.py execution engine. Frames are processed through parallel computer vision modules: deep learning object detection (YOLOv8) tracks vehicles and pedestrians; optical contouring detects road cavity depths; and dual LAB+HSV color-space segmentation measures standing water surface ratios.

Step 3: Real-Time In-Cabin ADAS Safety Interventions

When high-risk hazards are detected in the vehicle's direct trajectory (such as school children crossing an un-signaled street or a high-speed vehicle overtaking recklessly), an immediate in-cabin audio-visual ADAS warning is rendered to the transit driver, preventing catastrophic collisions.

Step 4: Spatial-Temporal Clustering & Multi-Hazard Escalation

Individual incident detections are processed through an 85-meter Haversine spherical distance matrix. Co-located defects (e.g., an unpaved road with deep potholes, muddy waterlogging, and traffic congestion) are merged into unified incident clusters and escalated to Priority 98–100 CRITICAL, preventing disjointed departmental blame-games.

Step 5: Zero-Loss Storage, Depot Sync & Municipal Command Dispatch

Telemetry is stored in local, zero-latency CSV ledgers and JSON payloads. Raat me jab bus transit depot wapas aati hai, high-resolution evidence frames automatically offload via Depot Wi-Fi to the Central Municipal Headquarters, generating automated work orders for PWD, Jal Sansthan, and Traffic Police.

4. Deep-Dive: The 8 Core Detection & Telematics Modules

Each module in the ai_engine/ package is specialized to tackle a specific civic urban infrastructure challenge:

1. 🕳️ Smart Pothole & Road Cavity Engine (smart_pothole_detector.py)

• Working Mechanism: Uses OpenCV Morphological Blackhat filtering (closing minus original frame) to extract dark surface depressions on asphalt roads. It measures both contour area (px) and estimated depression depth (cm).
• False-Positive Suppression: Evaluates aspect ratio and fill-extent (>0.85) to distinguish real cavity depressions from smooth flat asphalt repair patches and surface tree shadows.
• Civic Output: Categorizes defects as MEDIUM (2.5–4cm), HIGH (4–6cm), or CRITICAL (>6cm) and routes an automated Cold-Mix Repair Ticket to PWD / NHAI.

2. 🚗 Traffic Density & Bottleneck Intelligence Engine (vehicle_detector.py)

• Working Mechanism: YOLOv8 Nano detects vehicles across 4 key classes (Cars, Motorcycles, Transit Buses, Commercial Trucks). ByteTrack assigns persistent tracking IDs to avoid recount duplication.
• Bottleneck Heuristics: Computes vehicle count surge combined with bus telemetry speed drop (<36 km/h). Flags dynamic choke points and notifies Integrated Traffic Management Systems (ITMS) for adaptive green signal prioritization.

3. 🌊 Dual-Channel Waterlogging Optics Engine (waterlogging_detector.py)

• Working Mechanism: Traditional RGB color thresholding fails on roads due to glare. Our engine implements dual-channel color spaces: LAB color space (L-channel) detects dark stagnant muddy water pools, while HSV space isolates specular sheen and glossy glint.
• False-Positive Suppression: Applies a horizontal Sobel gradient mask (grad_x) to detect and subtract straight white lane stripes and zebra crossings.
• Civic Output: Computes road surface water ratio (%), flags puddle counts, and generates emergency Suction Pump Work Orders for Municipal Jal Sansthan.

4. 🚶‍♂️ Pedestrian Safety & School Zone ADAS (pedestrian_detector.py)

• Working Mechanism: Detects pedestrian clusters in proximity to the vehicle's dynamic braking path. Identifies school children and vulnerable pedestrians.
• In-Cabin ADAS Safety Intervention: Emits high-priority audio-visual warning signals inside the driver's cabin when pedestrians enter the dynamic vehicle corridor, directly preventing blind-spot collisions.

5. 🔍 Corridor-Aware ANPR & Speed Offender Tracking (anpr_detector.py)

• Working Mechanism: Uses vehicle bounding box edge contouring to isolate license plate regions. Applies regional corridor state prefixes (e.g., UP-53 Gorakhpur, KA-04 Bengaluru, DL-01 Delhi, MH-02 Mumbai).
• Offender Detection: Compares vehicle spatial displacement against transit speed benchmarks. Vehicles exceeding 48 km/h on urban roads or 32 km/h in designated school zones are automatically logged for Traffic Police e-Challans.

6. 🗑️ Garbage Heap & Sanitation Monitoring (ai_engine)

• Working Mechanism: Scans roadside berms and pedestrian footpaths for illegal roadside solid waste dumps and overflowed municipal bins.
• Civic Output: Routes GPS-stamped cleanliness tickets with photographic verification to Nagar Nigam sanitation wardens under the Swachh Bharat Urban Mission.

7. ⚠️ Traffic Signal Fault & Blackout Detector (ai_engine)

• Working Mechanism: Detects intersection signal posts and verifies active lamp illumination states.
• Fault Alerts: Detects signal blackouts (zero active lights) and contradictory multi-lamp states (both red and green active), alerting the Traffic Signal Maintenance Division.

8. 🚌 Fleet Delay & Origin-Destination Transit Analytics (app.py & data/)

• Working Mechanism: Compares real-time GPS progression against scheduled timetable baselines.
• Transit Optimization: Pinpoints exact road segments causing recurring trip delays, allowing transit planners to optimize bus schedules and transit priority lanes.

5. Spatial-Temporal Clustering & Multi-Hazard Escalation Algorithm

One of the project's most powerful innovations is the Coordinated Spatial-Temporal Clustering Engine implemented in ai_engine/incident_generator.py.

In traditional municipal governance, different departments operate in silos: PWD fixes roads, Jal Sansthan manages drains, and Traffic Police manages jams. When a road breaks down, departments blame one another. Our algorithm solves this mathematically:

Algorithmic Step Mathematical / Logical Principle Operational Value
1. Spherical Distance Calculation Haversine Formula:
d = 2R · atan2(√a, √(1-a))
where a = sin²(Δlat/2) + cos(lat1)·cos(lat2)·sin²(Δlon/2)		 Calculates exact geodesic distance between geo-tagged defects on the Earth's curved surface without heavy GIS server dependencies.
2. 85-Meter Cluster Grouping Any hazards detected within an 85-meter GPS radius and overlapping transit window are assigned a unified Cluster_ID (e.g., CLUST-004). Eliminates duplicate incident logging when multiple buses scan the same road cavity or flood puddle.
3. Multi-Hazard Escalation If co-occurring hazards from multiple distinct categories are grouped into the same cluster (e.g., Severe Pothole + Water Pool + Traffic Choke), priority automatically escalates to Priority 98–100 CRITICAL. Triggers unified joint work orders, forcing PWD road teams, Jal Sansthan drainage jetting suction units, and Traffic Police wardens to respond cooperatively.
